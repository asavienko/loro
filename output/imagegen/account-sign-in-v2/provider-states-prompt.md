# Provider sign-in states

Mode: built-in imagegen. Visual concept only.

Use case: ui-mockup. Input image 1: reference and edit target for a companion sign-in states board.
The user points at the Google button in the first screen and requests screens showing sign-in
states. Create a high fidelity wide landscape board with FIVE fully visible, large and legible
mobile artboards side by side. Match the supplied Loro design exactly: warm ivory backdrop, charcoal
typography, burnt orange accents, white outlined rounded buttons, slim "Sign in & sync" spine, "‹
Today" link, the small two linked cards illustration, generous whitespace and rounded phone
boundaries. Board title "Sign-in, step by step"; subtitle "Loro · Google and Apple sign-in states".
This is a design concept, not an actual signed-in session. First four screens preserve the same
illustration, heading "Your progress, wherever you go" and body "Sign in to keep your phrases and
progress together across devices." Their THREE provider rows remain at the same vertical positions:
"Continue with Google" with Google logo, "Continue with Apple" with Apple logo, "Continue with
email" with envelope. Reserve an inline status area below these buttons so the layout stays stable.
Footer on all first four screens: orange link "Keep practising without an account", then "Your
progress stays saved on this device." Screen 1 caption "Ready": three enabled provider buttons, no
warning or status. Screen 2 caption "Connecting": Google button shows spinner and label "Connecting
to Google…"; Apple and email controls are visibly disabled with readable muted text. Reserved status
text "Complete sign-in in the secure window." Small enabled action "Cancel sign-in". No fake
provider login form inside the app. Screen 3 caption "Cancelled": all three provider buttons
restored and enabled. Neutral compact inline info notice below buttons reading "Sign-in was
cancelled. Choose a method to try again." No red alarming error treatment. Screen 4 caption
"Couldn’t sign in": all three provider buttons enabled. Gentle pale warm warning panel below buttons
with simple warning icon and text "We couldn’t sign you in. Please try again or use another method."
No technical codes, no duplicate retry button, the existing choices are the retry actions. Screen 5
caption "Signed in": same spine, Today link and linked-card illustration with small restrained
orange checkmark. Heading "You’re signed in". Email "you@example.com". Body "You can now sync your
phrases and progress across devices." One orange primary "Back to practice". Footer "Your progress
stays saved on this device." Do not imply progress already synced or invent sync counts. Small
board-level note beneath screen captions: "The same states apply to Apple sign-in." Keep it outside
the phones. Constraints: preserve the supplied visual identity, crisp readable English, identical
screen geometry, all five screens in frame, no Connection details, no email-address/code form on
this board, no password or third-party consent form, no fake numerical metrics, no test badges, no
developer overlay, no extra providers. Foreground buttons and copy should have good readable
contrast.
