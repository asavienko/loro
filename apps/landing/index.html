Memory

It comes back just before you’d forget.

Each rating goes into FSRS, an open memory model that runs in Loro’s Rust core, on your phone. It works out when a phrase is about to slip and plays it to you then — the same code on Android, iOS and the web.

  • MissedComes back at the end of this queue.
  • HardAlso back at the end of this queue, then scheduled.
  • EasyMoves on. The next review waits longer.

Three promises

What Loro won’t do.

  1. 01

    Nothing you say leaves your phone.

    You talk back to every phrase, out loud. Loro doesn’t record you and doesn’t ask for the microphone. There is no cloud speech recognition, now or later.

  2. 02

    Every number is real.

    Recall and progress come from what you actually did and from the real memory model. Nothing is estimated to look encouraging.

  3. 03

    A missed day is just a day.

    No streak to lose and no screen that scolds you. Come back whenever; Loro picks up where your memory is.
