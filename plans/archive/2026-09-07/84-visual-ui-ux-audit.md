## Findings

1. **Wide-screen reading and targeting:** learner screens and bottom sheets stretch to the entire
   1440px viewport. Today separates phrase text and its status by over 1000px; Add separates phrase
   text and its add affordance similarly. Constrain the learner shell and modal panel to one
   centered readable column, preserving full-width mobile layouts. Keep the workbench spacious.
2. **Input spacing:** Add's search placeholder touches the left border in the phone capture. Inspect
   its styles and provide deliberate horizontal padding.
3. **Practice clarity:** Stream exposes playback controls without audio, and Refrain prints
   implementation jargon (`audio lands with the native module`). Review the existing plan-55
   truthfulness work before deciding the appropriate learner-facing treatment.
4. **Toast overlap:** phrase-edit and tag-drill captures show toasts covering the bottom primary
   action. Review toast placement and available viewport space before final verification.
5. **Dead-end empty states:** the unknown-phrase route offers guidance but no actionable escape when
   opened directly. Stream's all-learned state also lacks a direct Add action.
