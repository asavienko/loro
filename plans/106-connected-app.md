## Outcome

A learner signs in with an email code (or Google where configured), browses Loro's sets and albums
served by the API, makes their own phrase sets, songs and covers with AI inside daily limits, and
keeps each private, shares it by link, or publishes it for everyone.

## Decisions

- **The API owns content.** Loro's pre-generated sets, phrases, the phrase bank and albums are
  seeded into PostgreSQL from `packages/content/v2/` when the API starts. The app ships no phrase
  content: it downloads its course pack from `GET /v1/library/pack`, keeps the last copy on the
  device for offline use, and says so when it has neither.
- **Two kinds of thing, two tabs.** _Phrases_ (sets of phrases, the listening loop) and _Music_
  (albums of songs sung from phrases, a separate player) are separate tabs with separate pages;
  _Create_ is the generation hub for both. A phrase set is never shown as an album.
- **Visibility.** Everything a learner makes is `private` (only them), `link` (anyone with its share
  code) or `public` (listed in Community). Loro's own content is public and read-only.
- **Limits.** Generation is signed-in only and counted per user per UTC day in PostgreSQL
  (`library_usage`): phrase decks, covers and songs each have a daily allowance (`LIMIT_*_DAILY`),
  plus caps on how many sets, albums and songs one account keeps. `GET /v1/library/usage` shows what
  is left; a spent allowance is `429 LIMIT_REACHED` with `resets_at`, and the app shows it before
  the learner asks.
- **Honest fallbacks.** Without a Claude key, phrase decks come from the phrase bank, covers are
  drawn by the server from the title (`provider: "pattern"`), and lyrics are assembled from the
  set's phrases. Without a music provider a song gets a synthesized demo instrumental and is
  labelled "Demo sound". The provenance is stored with the item and shown; nothing pretends to be AI
  or a real recording.
- **Covers are SVG**, written by Claude or drawn by the server, sanitized to a small allow-list of
  shapes (no scripts, links, images or text), and served from `GET /v1/library/covers/:id.svg`.

## Scope

1. [x] API: `library` module — schema, seed of pre-generated content, pack, sets CRUD, visibility
       and share codes, Community listing, saves.
2. [x] API: usage limits service and `GET /v1/library/usage`.
3. [x] API: generation — phrases (Claude or bank), notes (Claude), covers (Claude or pattern), songs
       (lyrics from phrases, Claude or assembled; audio from ElevenLabs Music or the demo
       synthesizer), albums.
4. [x] App: API client, account store and the sign-in flow (email code in onboarding and from
       Settings, sign-out, display name, allowances).
5. [x] App: content from the API (pack download, offline copy, first-launch failure screen).
6. [x] App: tabs Home · Phrases · Music · Create · Library; Community in Phrases and Music.
7. [x] App: Make a set on the server generator; save to the account; cover generation.
8. [x] App: songs — album pages, the music player (`expo-audio`), lyrics with meanings, song
       generation.
9. [x] App: sharing (visibility, share links, opening a shared link, saving others' items).
10. [x] Progress sync: `GET/POST /v1/library/progress`, merged on the device with revisions.
11. [x] Review pass (2026-09-30): account switch on a shared device, multi-tab refresh, signed song
        URLs, pack cache after sign-out, allowance refunds, unknown durations, orphaned audio.
12. [x] Phrases spoken by the server's voices (`/library/speech`, ElevenLabs when configured),
        played on web and native with the device voice as fallback.
13. [x] Reports of public items; three take an item out of Community.
14. [x] Android emulator: onboarding, the pack, covers, songs, email sign-in (Keystore), Create,
        phrase clips.
15. [ ] Left: iOS device run; live Claude (phrases, notes, covers, lyrics) with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`;
        live ElevenLabs Music songs; a live Apple sign-in (the button shows once the server has
        Apple credentials; none are configured locally); moderation beyond reports.
16. [x] Google sign-in on the web (the tab leaves and returns to `/account`) and on iOS/Android (an
        auth session returning to `loro://account` / `loro-dev://account`, PKCE through
        `expo-crypto`). Checked on the Android emulator up to Google's page, a cancelled page, and a
        mismatched return; a full Google sign-in needs a real Google account.
17. [x] Sign in with Apple beside Google wherever the server offers it (first on iOS, as the App
        Store asks of apps with Google sign-in), through the same provider flow.
18. [x] A set's page lists the songs sung from it (`GET /v1/library/sets/:id/songs`).
