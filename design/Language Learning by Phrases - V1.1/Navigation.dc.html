Law · N16 · the spine
Every screen has one 28px band under the status bar: the place on the left, what's running on the right. Both are tappable, both are words. It is rendered from the route table, so a new screen appears in the switcher the day it is declared — and no screen can be reached only by going back.
Ongoing is a place you can return to
A paused wave, a playing loop and a half-answered flow are all ongoing: they appear in the chip, in the switcher's first group, and on Today as a resume row. One list, three views — they can't disagree.
Per class
Root · Pushspine · place + chip
Sessionspine · leaving pauses
Flowspine · steps + finish
Sheetnone — dismiss first
Why words, not a tab bar
Five icons at the bottom would fix the app at five destinations and take the thumb arc the single filled control needs. The spine costs 28px at the top, names things in the product's own language, and grows with the route table instead of against it. It also swallows the old pull-down gesture: the caret is the affordance, the pull still works.
What changed, and where to look
The rail carries state and leaves the thumb
Four text buttons on a hairline under the header — Review 12 is a count, not a label. The bottom of the screen belongs to one filled control.
Home is the day, in order
Time · what happened · what's next, as hairline rows. Done rows recede to ink-6; the next thing is the only one at full weight. No tiles, no cards, no dark hero — the ambient loop is a row like everything else.
The CTA promises the cost
"5 phrases · about 3 minutes" under the label, so entering is an informed choice and fewer sessions need resuming.
Back says where it goes
‹ Today · ‹ Phrasebook. Arrival is stated once on a single dismissible line, which is also what makes a cold-start ✕ read as sensible.
Navigation is never more than one tap away
The spine's place name opens the switcher from root, push, session and flow alike; its chip returns you to the paused wave or the playing loop. No screen is a cul-de-sac.
The transport is a hairline, not a pill
Wave · phrase · position, with ❙❙ to pause and ✕ to end — two rules, two glyphs, no dark bar floating over the page.
Leaving offers pause first
Pause · End it here · Keep going — three plain choices in the product's voice, ordered by what a learner usually means. No "Progress lost".
More lists things
Lately · Phrases · Practice · You — recent first, counts where they help, no paths on screen. Unbuilt groups stay hidden rather than greyed.
One job per glyph
‹ back in a stack · ✕ leave a place · ⌄ reveal in place · ⋯ more of the same kind · ⌕ search. The Go pill is gone; search lives on ⌕ in More.
Nothing dead is offered
A rail item or More row appears only with content behind it, so early days show a shorter app rather than empty screens.
The rail is declared per home route, so resolveHome() can swap the surface underneath without touching this shape. Everything below is a state of this same system, not an alternative to it. Screen count: 14 hairlines, 4 filled controls across four screens, 2 cards — plus one spine, on all of them.

New law · N14
While a Sheet is up, the surface beneath it has no exit: the session's ✕ dims and stops responding, and the sheet's ✕, its scrim, and swipe-down all mean "dismiss the sheet". One escape at a time.
Why it can't be a route push
A Session has gestureEnabled: false; a Sheet has swipe-down. Presenting the paywall as presentation: 'modal' over the session keeps the session mounted, so audio and rep state survive (law 8) and no replace() is needed.
Accessibility
Focus is trapped in the sheet and the session is aria-hidden; the sheet's close control is labelled "Dismiss" — never "Close practice", which is the session's ✕.

Extension to N9
A link resolves to a route, a route resolves to a set, and an empty set is a state of that route — not a redirect. Every Session route with a ?source= owns an empty state whose copy names the source and offers exactly two ways on: fill the set, or do today's instead.
Who owns the copy
One line per PracticeSource kind, written once in the navigation module beside the parser — not thirteen times in thirteen screen plans. Deleted ids resolve to Phrasebook with the reason, because that is the nearest honest surface.
Testable today
Pure: (source, stream) => 'ready' | 'empty' | 'gone'. A table test over the seven source kinds, no renderer needed.
