Spine place + ongoing[] — the one persistent band. Left names the place and opens the switcher; right returns you to a paused wave, a playing loop or a half-answered flow. Two or more collapse to “2 ongoing”.
ScreenHeader variant root · push · session · flow — the variant declares the class, and with it the exit rule.
NavRail items[] with counts; an item appears only with content behind it.
SwitcherHandle · SwitcherSheet law N16 — one handle, same place on every class, rendered from the route table.
DayRow state done · next · idle. Exactly one row is at full weight.
TransportStrip tone hairline · dark. The only persistent chrome in the app.
ArrivalNote one dismissible line naming the entry point.
ExitSheet Pause · End it here · Keep going.
ResumeStrip law N13 — the home's CTA becomes Resume; it never grows a second button.
Laws the tokens encode
N13 a paused session leaves a resume mark, on the resolved home only.
N14 one escape at a time — the surface under a sheet has no exit.
N15 audio that outlives its route carries its transport.
