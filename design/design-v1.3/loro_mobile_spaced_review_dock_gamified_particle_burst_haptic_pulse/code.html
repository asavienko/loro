«Me pone...» Courtesy Formula3rd Pers. Present Indicative

Unlike the stiff imperative «Póngame» or English "Give me", native Spanish speakers in cafes employ the 3rd person singular present indicative: "¿Usted me pone...?" with the subject omitted. It translates literally as "Does your honor put/serve me...", radiating warm colloquial politeness.
