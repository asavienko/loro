# Vision

## One sentence

Loro teaches Spanish, Bulgarian and Russian by the phrase, by ear: you hear a phrase in your
language, say it in the pause, hear it in the language you're learning, and rate how it went; FSRS
decides when it comes back.

## Why phrases

1. **They're usable immediately.** "La cuenta, por favor" works on the first day.
2. **They carry pronunciation and melody.** A phrase has intonation; a word list doesn't.
3. **They're memorable as units.** Chunking is how fluent speakers store language.
4. **They make the parrot honest.** _Loro_ is Spanish for parrot: repetition until it's yours is the
   method.

## What Loro is

- **Listening-first.** The loop runs hands-free, like a playlist: sets of phrases, songs sung from
  them, one player.
- **Yours.** Learners make their own sets and songs (with AI, within daily limits) and keep them
  private, share them by link or publish them to Community.
- **Offline-first.** Progress lives on the device and syncs when signed in; course packs are kept
  for offline use.

## What Loro is not

- **Not a grammar course.** Grammar appears only as a note on a phrase you already care about.
- **Not a streak machine.** Nothing punishes a missed day.
- **Not surveillance.** Nothing the learner says is recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Not pretend.** Every number shown is real, and every AI or fallback item says what it is.

## Who it's for

An adult with a real reason to speak: a trip, a partner's family, a move. Not for learners who want
written exams or grammatical scaffolding.
