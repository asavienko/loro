# v2.0 design decisions

The v2.0 web prototype explored a listening-first Loro; on 2026-09-30 it became the app
(`apps/mobile`), and the prototype and its review rounds are in Git history at `52a0e3b`. These are
the decisions the app carries. They describe its behaviour; Product has not formally signed them
off.

Requirement IDs: `P3-01`, `P3-31`, `P2-24`, `P2-06`, `AI-06`, `LIB-03`, `LIB-04`, `F-04`, `F-08`.

## Decisions

| Vocabulary                | **Set** (a group of phrases), **Phrase**, **Song** (sung from a set's phrases) and **Album** (a collection of songs). No waves, packs, decks or drills.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Languages                 | Courses teach Spanish (`es-ES`), Bulgarian (`bg-BG`), British English (`en-GB`), Russian (`ru-RU`), American English (`en-US`), Polish (`pl-PL`) and Czech (`cs-CZ`); the app speaks British and American English, Bulgarian, Russian, Polish and Czech. A course is never in the learner's own language, and the two Englishes count as one language (an en-GB speaker is not offered en-US, nor the reverse), which makes 34 pairs. American English speakers read the English UI and the English notes, with American prompts and glosses (`translations`/`words` key `en-US`); a separate en-US UI copy was rejected as near-identical. Polish, Czech and American English were added on 2026-10-02, both as courses and as the learner's language, written by AI (Claude Sonnet 5.5) and awaiting native review ([Q-23](../decisions/open-questions.md#q-23)). The Polish UI addresses the learner as «ty», as Polish apps do; the Czech, like the Bulgarian and Russian, uses the polite plural (vykání). The API writes notes for learners' own Polish, Czech and American English phrases by spelling, as for English and Russian (`notes/pl.ts`, `notes/cs.ts`). The list comes from the API (`GET /v1/library/languages`). Every language the app speaks is taught too (2026-10-01): the server already had a voice for each, and English is what Bulgarian and Russian speakers most want; keeping English and Russian interface-only was rejected. The English and Russian courses were written by AI (Claude Sonnet 5.5) and await native review ([Q-23](../decisions/open-questions.md#q-23)). |
| Tabs                      | Home, Explore, Create and Library. Songs live in their sets, marked by a music-note icon, and play in the one player; albums are in Library ([plan 107](../../plans/archive/2026-09-30/107-one-player.md)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| The loop                  | Prompt in the native language → silence sized to the target → target; repeated; then, if the phrase has no rating yet, a short hold for one.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Recall rule               | The target text stays hidden (player, mini-player, queue, lock screen, the set's current row) until it has been heard. A phrase with no target clip offers to show the text instead.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| A recording being made    | A phrase whose recording the server hasn't made yet is made when first played (P3-01): the player shows a spinner and says the recording is being made and plays it as soon as it is ready; the mini player says "Making the recording…". No percentage or time is shown (the provider reports none). If it can't be made (or takes over a minute) the loop stops and says so, offering the target's text, as for a phrase with no recording. See [library.md](../architecture/library.md#phrases-spoken-by-the-server).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Grades                    | Missed / Hard / Easy → FSRS Again / Hard / Good. FSRS Easy is not offered. The grades show no intervals: FSRS schedules each review. Missed or Hard also plays the phrase once more at the end of the queue, marked Again; shuffling or adding to the queue keeps those last.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Rating window             | A rating can be changed or undone for five minutes, then counts at its original time. Statuses, due counts, review dates and Learned show it at once; points wait for the window.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Points                    | +1 per phrase listened (once per 5 minutes), +1/+2/+3 per Missed/Hard/Easy rating, +10 once when learned; derived from the log.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Learned                   | FSRS review state, stability ≥ 21 days, ≥ 3 successful recalls.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| First review              | The first successful rating comes back within 1 day if the phrase was first heard that day, or within 4 days if it was also heard earlier. Days are the learner's local days, stamped on each log entry, so every device replays one log to the same schedule in any time zone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Review date               | A phrase is due when predicted recall falls to 90%; the core's 50% date is the upper bound (open question [Q-24](../decisions/open-questions.md#q-24)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Repetitions               | Auto: 3 while new or last rated Missed or Hard, 1 under review; override 1 or 3.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Lock screen               | On iOS and Android the player plays on with the screen locked, on the lock screen and atop the notification shade: the prompt until the target is heard, play or pause, Next, and Missed / Hard / Easy, given as in the app and gone for the rating's window.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Time to say it            | The silence is 1.3× the target's measured length plus 0.6 s, within 1.5–8 s (Standard), or 2× plus 1 s, within 2.5–12 s (Longer). Until the target has been heard once, the first pause is estimated from its text; a duration on screen is only ever measured.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Say it again              | After the target plays, a short silent echo: the learner says the phrase once more, just as they heard it, before the next repetition (or the rating hold). It is the phrase's measured length plus 0.4 s, within 1.2–6 s (Standard), or 1.5× plus 0.8 s, within 2–9 s (Longer): shorter than the first turn, since the phrase is fresh. No cue sounds (the target just ended), the target text stays shown, and it is a fourth step on the player's track. Rejected: replaying the target a second time (more listening, not more speaking) and making the echo optional (one more setting for a few seconds per repetition).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| End of a queue            | A review (at most the 10 most overdue), the demo and a Library list play one pass and end on a panel. A set's queue follows the play-mode setting: play the queue again, or continue with the course's next phrases; a card atop the player says which, and the next set is a tap away (on the bar, before the grades).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Where rating happens      | In the full player and over the mini-player. Once a grade is given the grades hide for its window: the player shows the grade given with Undo; over the mini-player a tap rates, Undo stays 4 s with no message (moving on shows the next item's grades), then nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Gestures                  | Swipe the mini-player sideways: the next or previous item slides in beside it, its grades with it, as for Next and the loop moving on. Paused, the mini-player can be closed: its close button (beside Next, only while paused) or dragged down and let go far enough; a phrase queue empties (ratings and progress stay) and a song unloads. Playing, it can't be closed, so a stray drag never stops playback; the close is not undoable, as Play from any set starts again. Windows that slide up (the player, the queue, Make a set, the account, sheets) close when pulled down by their top.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Touch feedback            | On iOS and Android every control answers a touch with a haptic: a light tap for a button, row or card; a tick for a choice (a tab, an option, a chip, a like); a toggle for a switch; a click as a drag reaches the point where letting go acts (a card, a window pulled down, a swipe). Grades have none of their own: the rating's cue is theirs. iOS System Haptics and Android Touch feedback turn them off; the app adds no switch. The web has none.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Home                      | One hero, the first that applies: the demo, the review, Continue (the set in progress until every phrase of it has been started and none is due, then the next set), or the course done.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Make a set                | A topic, keywords or pasted text become suggested phrases, decided one card at a time (swipe or Add/Skip, Undo, correct before adding). Each card names its source: a course set, the learner's own, the phrase bank, or AI marked as unchecked by a native speaker. The learner can add their own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Phrase notes and pictures | Every phrase has a picture (one to three Material Symbols on its topic's colour, never a photograph), a mnemonic (a hook for remembering it: a sound-alike, a picture or a known word, never an invented fact), a grammar rule and its sounds (IPA and a respelling). A learner who wants a different mnemonic or grammar note asks for one with the button under it (2026-10-01): the app sends the phrase, its meaning, the language to write in and every note of that kind they already read, and the model is told the learner wants another, unlike those (`POST /library/generate/note`, from the day's phrase allowance). Each note written so is kept on the device by phrase, note and language (`shared/generate/noteCache.ts`), and the newest is shown first, the earlier ones and the phrase's own under it; while one is written the app names the language it is written in. The sounds are not rewritten (IPA is a fact, not a hook), and without a model there is no second note, since the written rules have one per phrase; keeping the notes on the server was rejected as more than a convenience needs.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Make a song               | From Create or an album: a set, one of twelve styles (pop, folk, ballad, sing-along, rock, hip-hop, reggaeton, jazz, dance, country, lullaby, bossa nova), an album and a title; then the lyrics first ([plan 113](../../plans/113-lyrics-first-songs.md)): written by AI in the background and shown line by line with meanings, to be written again or changed as the learner asks in their own words (each writing one of the day's lyrics), and only then sung ("Sing it"). Without an AI writer the lyrics are the set's phrases as they are, and there is nothing to rewrite. The song appears in its album as it is made; the app says when it is ready (a toast; a notification on the phone when the app is in the background or closed), and a tap on it opens the album.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| A song's lyrics           | What was sung: a sung song is heard back on the server and each line is shown as sung, with the time it starts (a tap goes there) and its meaning; a line the singer changed shows the written line under it, a line the singer skipped says so. The demo sound's lines are timed by its bars. A song that couldn't be heard back shows its lyrics as written, untimed; no timing is ever estimated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Covers                    | Drawn: a set's topic colour and icon, an AI illustration asked to carry no text ([ADR-0015](../architecture/adr/0015-open-model-providers.md)), or shapes (written by AI or drawn from the title) that only the server renders to SVG. Never photos, never text. A button on the artwork of any set, album, song or phrase draws a new one; its sheet lets the learner say what to picture in their own words (starting from what they last asked, else the item's own words) and shows the covers they drew for it before, one tap putting one back without drawing or spending a cover ([library.md](../architecture/library.md)).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Visual system             | One light palette. Terracotta fill only for play and the primary action; a selected state is ink; flat rows, tonal cards, one hero per screen.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Compact screens           | Under 380 dp of width at 100% text (`apps/mobile/src/shared/ui/room.ts`): a foldable's cover screen, or large text. Words stay whole: a label shrinks or takes its own line rather than breaking, and decoration goes first (a smaller player picture, only the current step or tab named).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |

## Engineering

- **Scheduling through core-rs.** FSRS runs in `packages/core-rs` (the `LoroCore` native module, or
  WASM on the web). The app mirrors only `retrievability`, for display, with the same curve as
  `packages/core-rs/src/fsrs/scheduler.rs`. Two local policies sit on top, in
  `apps/mobile/src/shared/state/memory.ts`: the 90% review date and the first-review cap.
- **Event-sourced learner state.** An append-only log with device-scoped ids; memory and points are
  derived from it. How two copies merge is in [sync-protocol.md](../architecture/sync-protocol.md).
- **An explicit player statechart** whose allowed events per status are data
  (`apps/mobile/src/shared/state/chart.ts`).
- **Touch feedback through two wrappers.** Every control is pressed through
  `apps/mobile/src/ui/Press.tsx` (a tick for a tab, radio or toggle role, else a tap; `haptic`
  overrides) and switched through `Toggle.tsx`; the lint keeps React Native's `Pressable` and
  `Switch` to them, so a new control can't be silent by accident. `src/shared/ui/haptics.ts` does
  nothing in a browser (`navigator.vibrate` is a motor buzz, not a tap, and iOS Safari lacks it); on
  iOS and Android `src/platform/haptics.ts` uses the feedback generators and Android's own view
  haptic constants, which honour the system setting and need no vibration permission. Rejected: an
  in-app haptics switch, a second setting for what the system already offers.
